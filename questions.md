# Question List — MT Onboarding Stacko Game

Content for the web facilitator tool (see PRD.md). 45 regular questions keyed to the block numbers, plus a pool of 8 special questions drawn when the tower falls.

## Regular Questions (1–45)

1. If you could travel anywhere tomorrow, where would you go?
2. What's one thing people should know about you when working together?
3. What are you most excited about joining this Future Leadership Program?
4. What's your most random hidden talent?
5. Are you more of a planner or a spontaneous person?
6. What's one achievement you're secretly proud of?
7. If you could have dinner with any famous person, who would it be?
8. What kind of leader do you aspire to become?
9. What's your biggest pet peeve?
10. What's one thing you're passionate about outside of work?
11. If you could instantly master one skill, what would it be?
12. What do you hope to learn from your fellow participants?
13. What's your go-to comfort food?
14. What's one personal strength you can bring to this group?
15. If your life had a theme song, what would it be?
16. What's one experience that shaped who you are today?
17. Would you rather work independently or collaboratively? Why?
18. What's one thing on your bucket list?
19. What motivates you when things get difficult?
20. If you could switch lives with someone for a day, who would it be?
21. What's one thing you're looking forward to experiencing during this program?
22. What's your biggest green flag in a teammate?
23. If you could describe yourself in three words, what would they be?
24. What's one leadership quality you admire most?
25. Are you a morning person or a night owl?
26. What's one thing you'd like to improve about yourself?
27. If you could have any superpower, what would it be?
28. What does success mean to you beyond job titles and money?
29. What's one fun fact about yourself that people wouldn't expect?
30. How do you usually handle disagreements with others?
31. If you could give your younger self one piece of advice, what would it be?
32. What's one thing you hope to contribute to the Future Leadership Program?
33. What's the most spontaneous thing you've ever done?
34. Who has influenced your personal or professional growth the most?
35. If our group had a team name, what would you suggest?
36. What's one challenge you're excited to take on during this program?
37. Would you rather be the smartest person in the room or the most creative? Why?
38. What's one value you would never compromise?
39. If you could learn from any leader in the world, who would it be?
40. What's something you've always wanted to try but haven't had the chance to?
41. What do you think makes a team successful?
42. What's one thing that instantly puts you in a good mood?
43. What kind of impact do you want to make through your future career?
44. If you were given the opportunity to lead a project tomorrow, what would you do first?
45. Complete this sentence: "By the end of this Future Leadership Program, I hope to…"

## Special Questions (S1–S8)

Drawn at random when the tower falls. Everyone in the group answers.

- **S1.** If our group were a company, what would our company name be?
- **S2.** Who in this group would most likely become a CEO? Why?
- **S3.** If we had to survive on a deserted island, what role would each person take?
- **S4.** Everyone gives one compliment to the person sitting next to them.
- **S5.** What is one thing you think everyone in this group has in common?
- **S6.** Describe your group in one word!
- **S7.** Everyone shares one thing they hope to achieve together during this program.
- **S8.** Create a team motto that represents your group.
